# BK7258 DAC hardware-EQ register-lifecycle verification

Date: 2026-08-15

## Scope

Verify a chip-first, AP-only extension of the existing BK7258 speaker-DAC
lower half.  The extension transports four banks of five raw signed-22
coefficients through a versioned private ioctl, applies them before stream
start, reads the defined hardware fields back, and deconfigures them before
DAC teardown.  The selected T5-Board contributes no EQ preset or new pin.

The bounded hardware test intentionally enables all-zero raw banks.  It is a
register ownership and lifecycle test, not evidence for a Q format, nonzero
coefficient packing, filter response, pass-through behavior, stability,
frequency response or audio quality.

## Implementation contract

- `CONFIG_BK7258_AUD_DAC_EQ` depends on the existing AP Audio lower half and
  excludes `AUDIO_MULTI_SESSION`.
- `BK7258_AUDIOIOC_SET_DAC_EQ` is `_AUDIOIOC(0x80)`.  Its 88-byte v1 payload
  contains `version`, `size`, `flags`, and `4 x 5` signed 32-bit transport
  words constrained to the hardware's signed-22 range.
- The lower half immediately deep-copies a valid payload.  It accepts changes
  only in `RESERVED` or `CONFIGURED` while no DAC/DMA/ring/worker resource is
  live; pre-reserve, malformed and running requests are rejected.
- Apply is ordered after DAC init/mute/gain/PA-off and before ring/DMA/start.
  Deconfiguration is ordered after worker/DMA/DAC/PA quiescence and before DAC
  deinit.  Both paths verify the defined enable/coefficient fields through
  MMIO while the DAC resource is still owned.
- SDK/readback cleanup failure retains EQ and DAC ownership for explicit
  `SHUTDOWN`/`RELEASE` retry.  This path was reviewed but not fault-injected.
- `AUDIO_FU_EQUALIZER` remains unadvertised.  The software `libeq.a` and
  legacy undefined `bk_aud_eq_init/deinit` interfaces are not linked.

## Pre-flash and download evidence

The apps-only flow first performed a non-halting J-Link read of the packaged
BL1/BL2 public identity locations.  All three public fingerprints matched and
the result recorded `writes_performed=false` before the loader was invoked.
COM4 was not opened.

COM3 wrote exactly:

- CP `app_crc_flash.bin@0x11000-0x3f000`, range
  `[0x011000, 0x050000)`;
- AP `app1_crc_flash.bin@0x165000-0x2b000`, range
  `[0x165000, 0x190000)`.

Both erase/write operations reported PASS and `Writing Flash OK`.  BL1, BL2,
manifests, slot B, LittleFS, configuration, calibration and the official tail
were preserved.  After reboot, the target was observed at the preserved BL2
hold with PC `0x280205a2`, `VTOR=0x28020000` and release word zero.  The
RAM-only `JLNK` token then released the signed pair.

## Audio and EQ hardware result

Two complete non-halting reads of the 436-byte v6 diagnostic at `0x28054804`
were word-for-word identical:

- magic/version/size `BDCV`/6/`0x1b4`, terminal `PASSED`, result 0,
  `DIAGNOSTIC`, cycles 2;
- 63 submitted APBs, 63 dequeues, two completes, one explicit `STOP` and one
  natural `FINAL` drain;
- 40,320 submitted bytes and 35,840 played bytes;
- DMA alloc/start/stop/deinit/free, DAC start/stop and PA on/off all 2/2;
- 58 DMA completions, two final drain frames, underrun 0 and first-underrun
  snapshot all zero;
- driver state `RESET`, first error 0, resources 0 and final PA state OFF;
- producer priority 246 and worker priority 245 on AP CPU0, with semaphore
  backlog maximum/events both zero;
- DMA interval minimum/maximum `0x00927c00`, exactly 20 ms at 480 MHz;
- worker-service maximum `0x00270480` and callback maximum `0x00213d74`, both
  below one frame;
- validator tick regression, sampled `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` regression, sample
  errors and driver tick regression all zero;
- two successful Audio vote/releases and two observed AP-primary
  `120 -> 480 -> 120 MHz` transitions.

EQ-specific results were:

- pre-reserve/invalid/running rejection totals `2/12/2`;
- accepted configuration/apply/deconfigure/readback totals `4/2/2/4`;
- readback failures 0;
- caller-buffer overwrite after acceptance did not change the retained hash;
- expected and final driver configuration hashes both `0xd4ce7cad`;
- final shadow-valid/requested/applied flags and resources all zero.

The direct post-release EQ control register was `0x00000001`, so enable bit 2
was clear.  Powered-down bank-register raw values are not interpreted as
coefficient evidence; the four successful driver readbacks occurred before
DAC deinit while the hardware resource was owned.

## CP one-shot PM and liveness result

Two CP PM diagnostic reads were identical: attempts 273, entered 1, aborted
272, wakeups 1, final reason `ENTERED`, sleep 21,375 us and two compensated
whole ticks.  One-shot counters then remained frozen.

The fixed-clock route register was `0x600807c8`, with CP bit 29 and AP-primary
bit 30 set.  CP SysTick CTRL/RVR were `3/0x13f`; CVR changed from `0xa6` to
`0x4b`.  Terminal DVFS registers represented the released 120 MHz tier.
AP was READY, CPU2 was SCHEDULER_ONLINE, RPTUN was CONNECTED with flags
`0x7f3f`, and all three heartbeats advanced between the two snapshots.

## Verification boundary

- This result proves the private zero-bank enable/readback/deconfigure
  lifecycle and that it preserves the existing bounded Audio/PM result.
- It does not prove nonzero coefficient packing, a hardware Q format,
  transfer-function convention, stability, filtering, pass-through behavior,
  frequency response, acoustic output or software `libeq.a` behavior.
- Immediate failure rollback was not injected.  The source retains ownership
  for retry, but this record does not claim that hardware path executed.
- The ioctl is private and single-session.  It is not a standard NuttX
  equalizer ABI and must not be advertised as `AUDIO_FU_EQUALIZER`.
- The Audio tuple and timing boundaries remain those of the v5 baseline.  P6
  has no fitted speaker, so register lifecycle is the strongest automatic
  board evidence available without an external instrument.
