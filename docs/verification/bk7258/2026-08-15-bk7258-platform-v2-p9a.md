## Implemented contract

- `board/bk7258/scripts/bk7258_shadow_ledger.json` is a strict
  `bk7258.shadow/1` 27-row mapping bound to the frozen P0 manifest and
  migration-ledger digests.  Allowed row statuses are exactly `EXACT`,
  `EQUIVALENT_WITH_REASON`, `MIGRATION_PENDING`, and `RETIRE_PROPOSED`.
- `shadow-check` iterates every frozen profile and reports old metadata,
  resolved defconfig, source closure, device nodes, resource claims, SDK role,
  and package-plan evidence.  Unavailable fields remain explicit nulls; no
  row is reported as equivalent or exact without evidence.  The current 27
  rows are all `MIGRATION_PENDING` with rationale.
- `framework-check` covers P0-P8 metadata/schema invariants and generates
  representative T5 and AIDK plans/package metadata plus a dry transport plan.
  It is bounded and does not invoke a production build or test campaign.
- The release checklist records fork/PR separation and the later, scrubbed
  local-staging-to-`logs/` export gate.  No logs were exported or created.
